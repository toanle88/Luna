# Community Code of Conduct

Luna welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific when giving feedback.
- Discuss code and behavior rather than attacking people.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or disclose another person's private information.
- Treat health information carefully. Use synthetic examples instead of personal cycle data.
- Keep discussions relevant and respect maintainers' decisions about project scope.

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/toanle88) or ask for a private contact without publishing
sensitive details. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, or limit
participation. Reports are handled as privately as the available channel allows; no response-time
guarantee is made.
